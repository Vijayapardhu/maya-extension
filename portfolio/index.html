About

Small tools, carefully built.

I'm Vijayapardhu — also written as Vijaya Pardhu. I'm a developer who likes software that does one job well and then gets out of the way.

Most of my work lives in the browser: extensions, interfaces, and utilities that remove repetitive steps. I prefer vanilla JavaScript, minimal dependencies, and code that is easy to inspect, change, and learn from.

When I'm not building, I'm usually reading about browser internals, accessibility, and interface design.
